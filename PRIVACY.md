# Privacy policy — Zoziologie Web Tools

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

Zoziologie Web Tools adds export and navigation features to eBird, Macaulay Library, and supported Biolovision websites. It does not operate a separate server, require an extension account, or include advertising or analytics code.

## Information handled by the extension

The extension reads relevant content on supported birding pages to create exports and add links. Depending on the feature, this can include bird observations, species names, checklist or trip information, observer names, and observation locations. CSV exports are created in your browser and saved through the browser's download system. Copy-link controls write the selected observation link to your clipboard.

When you open the toolbar menu, the extension reads the address of the current tab to show which tools apply to it and whether the extension is running there. The address is not stored or sent anywhere.

If you click **Allow downloads** in the toolbar menu, the browser grants the optional downloads permission. The extension then uses it only to save the Macaulay Library photo or sound you click, under its ML number. It does not read or change your download history.

Your settings (which tools are turned off) are saved in the browser's extension storage. If browser sync is turned on, your browser syncs them with your other devices through your browser account, like other extension settings. They contain no birding data.

When you use a search or export control, the extension may request region suggestions, trip details, barchart data, or grid information directly from the birding website providing that feature. Macaulay media links open Cornell's media delivery service. These requests are needed to provide the selected feature. The websites handle their own accounts, cookies, and requests under their respective privacy policies.

## Collection and sharing

The extension does not send exported birding data, page contents, browsing history, or search queries to the developer. It does not sell user data or send it to advertising services. It does not read passwords or payment information. It does not store an extension-side copy of your exports or maintain a database of your activity.

The browser manages downloaded files and the clipboard independently of the extension. Files you download remain on your device until you move or delete them. Any later sharing of those files is under your control.

## Website access

Access to the supported websites lets the extension read the relevant page content and add its controls. You can restrict this access or remove the extension through your browser's extension settings (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).

## Contact and changes

For questions or issues, use the [project's GitHub issues](https://github.com/Zoziologie/zoziologie-web-tools/issues). Avoid including private observation data or account details in a public issue. Material changes to the extension's data practices will be reflected in this policy.
