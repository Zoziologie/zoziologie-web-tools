# Privacy policy — Zoziologie Web Tools

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

Zoziologie Web Tools adds export and navigation features to eBird, Macaulay Library, and supported Biolovision websites. It does not operate a separate server, require an extension account, or include advertising or analytics code.

## Information handled by the extension

The extension reads relevant content on supported birding pages to create exports and add links. Depending on the feature, this can include bird observations, species names, checklist or trip information, observer names, and observation locations. CSV exports are created in your browser and saved through Chrome's download system. Copy-link controls write the selected observation link to your clipboard.

When you use a search or export control, the extension may request region suggestions, trip details, barchart data, or grid information directly from the birding website providing that feature. Macaulay media links open Cornell's media delivery service. These requests are needed to provide the selected feature. The websites handle their own accounts, cookies, and requests under their respective privacy policies.

## Collection and sharing

The extension does not send exported birding data, page contents, browsing history, or search queries to the developer. It does not sell user data or send it to advertising services. It does not read passwords or payment information. It does not store an extension-side copy of your exports or maintain a database of your activity.

Chrome manages downloaded files and the clipboard independently of the extension. Files you download remain on your device until you move or delete them. Any later sharing of those files is under your control.

## Website access

Access to the supported websites lets the extension read the relevant page content and add its controls. You can restrict this access or remove the extension through Chrome's extension settings.

## Contact and changes

For questions or issues, use the [project's GitHub issues](https://github.com/Zoziologie/zoziologie-web-tools/issues). Avoid including private observation data or account details in a public issue. Material changes to the extension's data practices will be reflected in this policy.
